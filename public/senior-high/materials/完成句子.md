# 完成句子

## 2007年

### 2007年湖北卷

根据括号内的汉语提示，用句末括号内的英语单词完成句子。
They sat together around the table, with ____ (门关着). (shut)

#### 第31题

答案：the door shut

I haven't the slightest idea ____ (他正在说什么). (talk)

#### 第32题

答案：(of) what he's talking about

Last night, John was answering the letters that ____ (寄给他的) during the past two weeks. (arrive)

#### 第34题

答案：had arrived for him

He believes that children ____ (应允许……学习) at their own pace. (allow)

#### 第35题

答案：should (ought to/must) be allowed to learn (study)

She has an excellent ____ (对名字的记忆力), which helps her quite a lot in her work. (memory)

#### 第36题

答案：memory for names

____ (他是否出过国) doesn't make much difference. (he, abroad)

#### 第37题

答案：Whether he has been abroad or not

The factory's output of cars this year is ____ (大约是去年的三倍). (as, great)

#### 第38题

答案：about three times as great as that of last year

Not only ____ (要帮助) the disabled to find jobs, but also medical treatment will be provided for those who need it. (give)

#### 第39题

答案：will help be given to

It is possible that the King of Stonehenge was linked to the stones: he ____ (可能参与) in planning the monument, or in helping transport and pull up the stones. (hand)

#### 第40题

答案：may (might/could) have had a hand

### 2008年湖北卷

答案：The three longest rivers

答案：hadn't forced me

- No, I'll do it myself. (wash)

#### 第36题

答案：have / get your car washed

With the rapid development of science and technology, I can't imagine _________ (我的家乡会是什么样子) in ten years. (what)

It is your efforts, not your intelligence _________ (决定) your success. (determine)

________ (正如我们强调的那样) many times, "serve the people" is our first policy. (stress)

## 2014年

### 2014年湖北卷

根据汉语提示，用句末括号内的英语单词完成句子。
The community college has to cut down on staff ________. (save)
（为了省钱，那所社区学院只好裁员。）

#### 第71题

答案：(in order/so as) to save money
解析：考查不定式作目的状语。

So far the well-known journalist ________ more than 4,000 interviews with famous people. (accumulate)
（迄今为止这位著名记者采访名人已累积达四千余人次。）

#### 第72题

答案：has accumulated
解析：考查现在完成时，so far常与完成时连用。

Sometimes ________ to use examples to explain abstract concepts. (helpful)
（有时候举例也许有助于解释抽象的概念。）

#### 第73题

答案：it may/might be helpful
解析：考查it作形式主语，真正主语为不定式。

The police will reward ________ useful information to catch the robber. (provide)
（任何人提供有用信息帮助抓住劫匪，警方将予以奖励。）

#### 第74题

答案：whoever/anyone who provides
解析：考查宾语从句/定语从句，whoever指"任何人"。

He's the only student in the class ________ to take part in the Model United Nations conference. (select)
（他是班上唯一被选上去参加模拟联合国大会的同学。）

#### 第75题

答案：(who has been) selected
解析：考查定语从句/过去分词，student与select为被动。

No other technological development has had ________ as the growth of electronics on so many aspects of social, economic, and cultural development. (impact)
（没有哪项技术的发展像电子技术这样，对我们的社会、经济以及文化等诸多方面产生过如此重要的影响。）

#### 第76题

答案：such a great/important impact
解析：考查such...as结构，"如此……的影响"。

You ________ the meeting to tell me that; you could have come to tell me afterwards. (interrupt)
（你不该打断会议来告诉我那件事，你本可以会后告诉我。）

#### 第77题

答案：shouldn't have interrupted
解析：考查情态动词+have done，"本不该做却做了"。

This novel was once the ________ book in high schools in the United States. (read)
（这部小说曾经是美国高中阅读最广泛的书。）

#### 第78题

答案：most widely read
解析：考查最高级和副词，read的过去分词作定语。

The Public Square is an eye-catching sight of the city. ________ many stone sculptures of famous historical figures. (stand)
（大众广场是这个城市引人注目的景点，许多历史名人的石雕像矗立在那儿。）

#### 第79题

答案：There stand
解析：考查完全倒装，There stand位于句首。

It was several minutes ________ what he was saying. (take)
（过了好几分钟，我才理解他说的是什么。）

#### 第80题

答案：before I understood/made sense of
解析：考查It was...before...句型，"过了多久才……"。
